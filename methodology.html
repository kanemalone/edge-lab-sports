METHODOLOGY

How Edge Lab measures an edge.

Edge Lab will compare a model-derived probability with the market-implied probability for each available market. Sport and racing models will be evaluated separately because the relevant inputs, data quality and market structures differ.

Core calculation

Implied probability → derived from market price, with margin handled where the required market data is available.

Model probability → estimated probability from the sport-specific model.

Edge → model probability minus market-implied probability.

Expected value → derived only after probability, price and market assumptions are validated.

Validation

Track calibration, closing-line movement, realised results, sample size and model drift. Do not interpret a single signal as proof of an exploitable advantage.
