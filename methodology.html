METHODOLOGY

How Edge Lab measures an edge.

Edge Lab separates the market layer from the model layer. V0.2 collects event and bookmaker prices first; sport-specific probability models are added only after their inputs and historical performance can be tested.

Core calculation

Raw implied probability = 1 ÷ decimal odds.

Market fair probability = de-vigged selection probability when the complete market is available.

Model probability = sport-specific estimated probability.

Edge = model probability − market fair probability.

Expected value = (model probability × decimal odds) − 1.

Validation

Track calibration, closing-line movement, realised results, sample size, market liquidity and model drift. A displayed edge is an estimate, not a guarantee of profit.
